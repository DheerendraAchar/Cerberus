ℹ️ About Cerberus

What is an Adversarial Attack?

Adding tiny imperceptible noise to images to fool AI models. The model sees garbage, humans see normal images.

adversarial_image = original_image + small_noise
What is Adversarial Training?

Training model on both clean images AND adversarial examples. Makes it robust against attacks.

train_on(50% clean + 50% adversarial)
Transfer Attack:

Attack trained on one model also fools other architectures! But effectiveness varies.

ResNet attack → 83.7% success on ResNet
ResNet attack → 66.6% success on VGG

Finding: 17.18pp gap = architectural diversity is a defense!
